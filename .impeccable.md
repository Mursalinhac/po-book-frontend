## Design Context

### Users
Dealership sales staff building Corvette purchase orders at a desk or on a showroom tablet, often with the customer beside them. Job: pick model/trim, add options, enter customer + trade-in, review totals, print the PO.

### Brand Personality
Showroom luxury: precise, confident, quiet. Corvette/Ciocca identity, authentic vehicle imagery, catalog prices, field names/order and legal text are preserved untouched.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark showroom as the signature, with an equally considered light theme (system/light/dark selector stays). Vehicle imagery large. Primary actions and selection use neutral ink; red is reserved for errors and missing information. Soft depth via tone steps, not borders or heavy shadows. No gradients-as-decoration, no glass panels, no uppercase tracked micro-labels.

### Design Principles
1. The car is the hero; UI recedes around it.
2. Speed of entry for staff beats ornament: clear fields, big touch targets (44px+), visible focus.
3. Numbers are sacred: tabular figures, exact prices, totals always visible.
4. Print output stays faithful to the legal PO.
5. Primary actions and selection use neutral ink; red is reserved for errors and missing information.

### Rule precedence (skills conflict; resolve in this order)
User answers > README preservation list > impeccable + redesign audit > taste-skill house rules > soft-skill.
- App UI, not a landing page: fixed rem type scale, Geist only (bundled), no scroll reveals on form fields, no image/motion quotas.
- Taken from soft-skill: custom easing tokens, button-in-button trailing icon on primary CTAs, nested shell/core on home model cards.
- Rejected: glass/pill floating nav, eyebrow labels, gradients, resting shadows, side-stripe borders, 1px lines as section separators (tone steps instead; form fields and real tables keep borders).
- Icons: lucide with strokeWidth 1.5.

### Tokens (src/app/globals.css)
Surfaces: --page, --surface, --surface-raised, --surface-sunken. Text: --fg, --muted-fg. --line (fields/tables only). --accent-color, --accent-ink, --error.
Motion: --ease-out, --ease-spring, --dur-fast, --dur. Radius: --radius-shell, --radius-core, --radius-control. --shadow-float (floating elements only).
